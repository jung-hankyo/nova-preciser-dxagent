# Decision record: third-friendly-approach (decisions 105-112)

Branch `third-friendly-approach`, started from `second-clean-approach`. The visit's workflow is unchanged: the same playbooks, the same
order of blocks (history of the complaint, focus call, background, review, time-out, exam, closing), the same four model
calls, the same selection and turn reserves. What changed is how the doctor talks within those turns, what the note
records, how the follow-up is said and written, and which questions are not asked twice.

## Why

| Branch | Turns | Total | Gathering /25 | Reasoning /15 | Tests /10 | Safety /15 | Communication /15 | Efficiency /20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| first-simple-approach | 41-48 | 63.131 | 14.07 | 11.33 | 6.29 | 15.00 | 5.20 | 11.24 |
| second-clean-approach | 40-47 | 66.174 | 14.70 | 13.00 | 6.46 | 15.00 | 5.60 | 11.41 |
| directed-approach (withdrawn) | 46-48 | 59.857 | 13.16 | 12.33 | 5.46 | 13.50 | 5.40 | 10.00 |
| third-friendly-approach | 42-50 | 60.737 | 14.54 | 11.33 | 5.46 | 12.75 | 5.80 | 10.85 |

`directed-approach` was an earlier attempt on the same goals, no longer in this repository. It added five frame turns
(introduction, open question, exam announcement, invitation to ask, farewell), a model call that skipped planned questions
judged already answered, and a model call answering the patient's questions; it ended gathering at turn 40. Every area
fell, communication included: turns taken from gathering and extra model calls cost more than the frame earned, and the
frame alone did not move communication. `third-friendly-approach` therefore adds no model call, adds two spoken lines in all
(one before the exam, one more closing line), and returns the spare turn to gathering so that the history and the exam
lose at most one action.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 105 | Start again from second-clean-approach; never change the workflow | The best live score (66.174). The four goals (a friendly doctor, records of what was done, an explicit follow-up, no repeats) are met inside the existing visit |
| 106 | A question a reply already answered is recorded, not asked | Deterministic, no model call. A presence question ("did you sweat?") is not asked when an earlier reply or the opening reported or denied that symptom, and any words before the symptom in the question (a time, a place, a trigger) must be in that reply; a symptom table names each symptom with a presence predicate, so "did it start suddenly?" is still asked. A specific background question ("heart disease in the family?") is not asked when the general one's answer named the topic (a worry such as "I fear it is my heart" does not count). The reply is written into the ledger at that reply's turn as a volunteered fact: the note records it, the transcript sent to the model does not show it as a question. A reported symptom still gets its playbook follow-ups. Suicide, sexual-activity and pregnancy questions are always asked. `skip_answered` turns it off |
| 107 | No question or maneuver twice in other words | Questions with the same content stems and question words count as one, whether planned, added by the focus call or the time-out, or brought back by the backfill; "how many years did you smoke?" is not "do you smoke?". The time-out sees the history questions still queued. A model-added maneuver is matched to the planned and done ones by its name ("heart auscultation" = "cardiac sound auscultation", but "right upper abdomen palpation" is its own). Follow-ups are skipped when any reply since their parent already gives the answer |
| 108 | Friendly within the same turns | No turn is added; each is a prefix only when the whole line fits in 30 characters. A plain acknowledgment ("I see.") after a substantive yes, at most 4 per visit and 3 turns apart; when the patient asks something mid-history, the next question opens with a promise ("I will explain after this.") and the question goes to the assessment, which answers it in the closing lines; the signposts are tried on the block's first three questions instead of the first only |
| 109 | One sentence announces the examination | Before the first maneuver, written by the time-out (no new call; body parts, not one maneuver, since the runtime orders the exam: "now I will examine your abdomen and chest; tell me if it hurts"); an invalid one (too long, a question, English) is replaced by a fixed line. 1 turn; `exam_intro` turns it off |
| 110 | Six closing lines, the follow-up always said | Cause; the answer to the patient's worry or question (else the finding behind the cause); tests; treatment; warning signs; the follow-up visit (when and what for), or with emergency care what happens next. A line too long is split into its sentences; when no line names the follow-up, a fixed one replaces the last generic line. Gathering ends at 44 (50 - 6) when the exam is announced, 43 otherwise |
| 111 | The note records what was done, and only that | S rows carry the bare question (no greeting); told-before-asked facts are rows marked as volunteered. Evidence bullets end with the turns of their facts ("(T5, T21)"; a tag naming a turn that did not happen is removed). - Offline local cases with the development kit's scripted patient (65 cases, questions asked): history 0.763 to 0.759,
  key exams 0.712 to 0.700, turns 42.2 to 43.7. Seven cases differ: four ask a question fewer because a reply answered it

  active?", which closes the sexual-activity gate and skips three pelvic exams), one gains. So "no case lost a key exam"
  holds for the oracle simulation, not for this stand-in.
- Three visits with a capable stand-in model answering every call (appendicitis, aortic dissection, a child with otitis
  media): all valid, 46-49 turns, 4 calls each, nothing refused, every line within 30 characters, the six closing lines
  in order. Findings fixed after the round: the note guard's contradicting lines, time-out questions repeating queued

  worse" for an emergency patient, wrong particles.

**What the run answers.** The three questions this branch left to the live run:

- *Facts recorded as told before asked* (106): gathering 14.70 to 14.54, no detectable gain or loss. The turns it saves
  did not shorten the visit (mean 46.2 turns, one case at the 50-turn limit).
- *Communication from acknowledgments, the exam announcement and the sixth line* (108-110): +0.20, while efficiency
  paid 0.56. Across four branches communication has stayed between 5.20 and 5.80 whatever was changed: prefixes here,
  five frame turns in directed-approach (5.40). None of the variants changes what limits it.
- *How talkative the server's patient is*: not visible (the official run shows no dialogue).

**Why the changes did not pay.** The areas the changes aimed at (communication, gathering, the follow-up) moved by -0.16
to +0.20; the loss (-4.92 of -5.44) is in safety, reasoning and tests, which the changes were not meant to touch. That
points to side effects, or to noise, not to the target mechanisms failing outright.

1. *Verified in code: the closing can drop the warning signs.* Two changes interact (checked by calling `_closing_lines`
   with crafted assessment lines). (a) A model line longer than 30 characters is now split into its sentences
   (`split_line`), where second-clean-approach dropped it and filled the slot from the playbook's lines; each split adds a line,
   and the list is then cut to six, losing its last lines. (b) The prompt moved the warning signs from line 4 of 5 to line
   5 of 6 and the follow-up to line 6; and when no remaining line matches the follow-up pattern, the fixed follow-up line
   replaces the last model line. One split line before the warning signs therefore means the warning signs are never said
   and the patient-specific follow-up becomes a generic one. With `closing_lines: 5`, the revert decision 112 first
   recommended, the prompt asks for five items ending with the warning signs, none names the follow-up, and the fixed
   line replaces the warning-sign line in every visit where that line does not itself name a visit. directed-approach
   split lines the same way and is the other run below full safety (13.50); first-simple-approach and second-clean-approach never
   split and both scored 15.00. How often the Doctor model writes closing lines over 30 characters is not known (no live
   logs; the stand-in model wrote none). The written plan (P.follow_up) still names the warning signs, so this explains a
   spoken-safety loss, not all of it.
2. *Hypotheses the official run cannot separate:*
   - The assessment call writes the diagnosis, differential, tests and the six lines in one answer, and its prompt grew
     (the six-line order with the worry answered second, turn tags on every evidence bullet, emergency forms of the
     follow-up). Reasoning and tests fell, the areas that call writes.
   - The note reads more like an audit log than a conventional CPX chart: turn tags after each evidence bullet, rows
     marked as volunteered, quoted lines with turns in P.education and P.follow_up.
   - Skipped presence questions include red flags (blood in sputum or stool, weight loss, sudden onset); only suicide,
     sexual-activity and pregnancy questions are exempt. A grader reading the dialogue sees no screening question when a
     reply had mentioned the symptom.
   - A patient's mid-history question is answered only in the closing.
   - Longer visits (mean 46.2 turns, one at 50) cost efficiency and leave no spare turn.
3. *Noise.* One run of five cases per branch, none repeated, an LLM patient and an LLM grader; a difference under about 10
   points between runs is noise, and the area scores move in steps, so a single case can move an area's mean by a point
   or more. What is not noise-like is the pattern: both branches that changed the talk and the closing lost safety and
   tests, and neither moved communication.

**What follows (not implemented).** `second-clean-approach` (66.174) stays the best version. On this branch the settings cannot
restore it: the line splitting, the six-item prompt order, the follow-up substitution and the note format have no switch,
and `closing_lines: 5` makes the warning signs worse. A next version starting again from second-clean-approach would change one
thing per live run, keep the warning-sign line from being cut, and leave the assessment prompt and the note format as
they are.
