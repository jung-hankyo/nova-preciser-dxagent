# Decision record: the contest runtime (decisions 38-104)

Branch `first-simple-approach`, from the v0.4.2 core, October 2026. Releases: 0.5.0 (38-49) a runtime around the core, 0.5.1
(50-61) the official contract, 0.5.2 (62-71) the SDK and development kit, 0.6.0 (72-79) presentation playbooks, 0.7.0
(80-85) a lean visit, 0.7.1 (86-91) the revised rules, 0.7.2 (92-94) one action per maneuver, 0.8.0 (95-103) the evidence
review, 0.8.1 (104) a new Doctor model. Release notes: `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Problem

The contest differs from what v0.4.2 was built for. The Doctor model is fixed and reachable only through the contest
server, with no internet. Each turn is one action: SAY (at most 30 characters, one question), EXAM (one maneuver), TEST
(final round only). A case ends with a SOAP note and one primary diagnosis, and only what was asked and recorded counts.
v0.4.2 could not run there: its models are unreachable, it bundles up to 3 actions per turn, asks about 8 questions in
one English message, orders tests, ends on an exception without a diagnosis and writes no SOAP note. It also optimized
diagnostic accuracy, while most of the score depends on process and documentation.

## 0.5.0: a runtime around the unchanged core

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 38 | A separate runtime (`dxagent/nova`, `run.py`) driving the core nodes in a plain generator loop | Needs only pydantic and pyyaml; LangGraph and LangChain imports made lazy |
| 39 | One action per turn; no tests in the preliminary round | Core setting `allowed_action_types`; the runtime owns the turn budget |
| 40 | A Korean documentation checklist (HPI, background, ROS, standard maneuvers per complaint group) | Every item at most 30 characters, one question |
| 41 | Checklist answers read in one extraction call per block | Core mode only since 0.7.0 |
| 42 | LLM call diet | No contrast cards, fallbacks or prior assessor; at most 4 hypothesis calls; critic once |
| 43 | Final diagnosis policy `auto` | The core's leader if card-backed and at least 0.5, else the LLM's diagnosis |
| 44 | S and O only from the ledger, each item with its turn | LLM phrasing kept only if every number appears in the answers |
| 45 | Deterministic safety checks on the plan | Confirmatory and excluding tests; allergy-class removal; disposition raised for unstable vitals or a rapidly lethal primary; pregnancy check before radiation |
| 46 | Never fail a case | Per-case budget with a closing reserve; stop gathering 4 minutes before the limit; no LLM in the last 75 s; circuit breaker after 4 consecutive failures; any exception goes to closing and DIAGNOSE |
| 47 | The 8 fixture cards loaded by default | Superseded by 77 |
| 48 | Korean in the core | Findings and tests gain Korean names and questions; the lexicon matches Korean substrings; codes stay English |
| 49 | Closing explanation turns | SAY lines from the assessment, validated, with template fallbacks, quoted in P with their turns (five since 97) |

**Evidence** (`experiments/v05_protocol_sim.py`; held-out worlds, 2,000 paired cases per arm, scripted roles): one action per
turn costs -0.9 pp top-1 (CI -2.0 to +0.3); no tests costs -18.9 pp (-20.8 to -16.9). Without tests about half of the
cases end with the leader below 0.80, yet the truth is still in the final differential 98% of the time, so the
preliminary round's points are in the differential and in naming the deciding test in P. The size of the no-test effect
depends on how informative the simulated tests are.

## 0.5.1: the official contract

| # | Decision | Choice |

| 55 | Note structure | The SDK's sections from `empty_record()` when importable; a field is never added; byte limits enforced |

| 59 | Guideline library | Read at run time; titles indexed in `prepare()`; up to 3 documents and 2,400 characters in the assessment prompt; nothing derived is stored, compiled or shipped |

| # | Decision | Choice |

| 70 | Time | The budget is anchored to the session deadline (minus 15 s), not the process clock |

## 0.6.0: presentation playbooks

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 72 | Playbooks instead of more knowledge cards | Cases come from a finite list of CPX complaints and the examiner's checklist follows the complaint more than the hidden disease. A YAML file per complaint is the visit's deterministic floor: it works when the model returns broken JSON, a clinician can audit it, and it can be measured offline. On 18 unseen cases the fixture cards changed nothing |
| 73 | Selection | The playbook whose match word appears earliest in the opening; `not` words exclude; adverbs and durations are removed from a second copy of the opening; a word starting with a one-syllable noun must start a word. No match: the topic plan may name one from a menu |
| 74 | Merge with the model | The playbook supplies the HPI, background, ROS and up to 8 maneuvers (6 turns kept); the assessment gets the usual and can't-miss causes as a reference, not an answer |
| 75 | Content rules, enforced by the validator | Single-topic questions of at most 30 characters, positively phrased; known chart sections; standard maneuver names; at most 8 maneuvers; 2-4 safe closing lines; at least 3 differential entries |
| 76 | Yes/no after a negative question | A bare "yes" after a negatively phrased Korean question means "no", so it is left undetermined; an answer naming the absence still counts |
| 77 | Fixture cards off by default | `NOVA_USE_FIXTURES=0`: no history gain on unseen cases, 4 extra turns, chest-pain maneuvers in a short-stature visit |
| 78 | Model-free note | Without an assessment, the note lists the playbook's can't-miss causes as "r/o" lines, its tests and its closing lines |
| 79 | Review before use | Six independent AI reviewers, one per organ-system group; every finding adjudicated by hand (`docs/PLAYBOOK_REVIEW.md`). AI review is not clinical sign-off: every file stays `status: draft` until a clinician signs it |

## 0.7.0: a lean, patient-specific visit and a chart-shaped note

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 80 | The Bayesian core off by default | No cards ship, so its belief update and planner had nothing to work with, and its extraction, hypothesis calls, critic and question bank cost 9-10 of the 13 model calls per case and fed only prompts. `NOVA_CORE=1` restores it. Model usage is part of efficiency |
| 81 | One mid-visit focus call | After the priority-1 history: names the leading diagnoses, skips optional items that would not change the assessment, adds priority-3 items that now matter, writes up to 3 questions and 2 maneuvers; if it fails, the deterministic plan runs unchanged |
| 82 | Tiers and a turn target | Every item carries a tier (1 always, 2 unless skipped, 3 only if added); no filler: the visit ends when the plan is done. Cutting priority-2 items to a 40-turn target cost 10 single items across 18 cases (history 0.85 to 0.82); revised by 86 |
| 83 | Communication inside 30 characters | Prefixes only when they fit, one per utterance: greeting with the form of address; an acknowledgment from the opening; acknowledgments of voiced worry or pain (twice at most); signposts; a closing "what worries you most?"; closing lines on cause, next step and the worry. Only explanations said during the visit count |
| 84 | A chart-shaped note | Chief complaint with onset and pain score; template items in order with `( )`; the fixed ROS block; O.exam by region with turns; evidence bullets (an invented number drops the bullet); r/o lines with the separating test; P.tests by kind |
| 85 | Final-round tests stay in the core mode | The preliminary round has no TEST; test ordering keeps the core's value-of-information path (`NOVA_CORE=1`) |

## 0.7.1 and 0.7.2: the revised rules, one action per maneuver

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 86 | 40 turns is an aim, not a cut | Priority-2 items are cut only when the plan would run past the soft cap; the focus call is told the turns used, where the plan ends, the usual length and the limit. History 0.82 to 0.85, turns 39.3 to 42.2 |
| 87 | Wrongly sent actions cost a turn | An empty or over-long SAY/EXAM and the exam refusals each use a turn without an answer. The agent counts them, and `check_exam` refuses locally any EXAM that names a test, a treatment, a record or a question |
| 88 | Model-call parameters | The visit's roles send `reasoning_effort="medium"` (fewer empty answers when given explicitly); the core's stay "low". Temperature is left to the model |
| 89 | Waiting on a slow call | `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` and `REQUEST_PENDING` are resent under the same `request_id` after 5, 10, 20 and 30 s (a new request would start a second call); limits: 30 minutes per visit, 240 s per HTTP call |
| 90 | SDK 1.0.1 or later in the ZIP | The server refuses requests from 1.0.0 |
| 91 | Diagnosis and note, revised | The primary diagnosis is graded on a 5-point scale, so the assessment asks for the most specific diagnosis the findings support. The note: no `P.I:` prefix; short chart lines ending with the worry or request; chart-form onset; a growth region and one vital-sign line |
| 92 | One action per maneuver | The examiner refuses a request naming a symptom without its method, several parts or two measurements. Eighteen of our names became one specific action (30 playbooks; `docs/PLAYBOOK_REVIEW.md`); model-written maneuvers must be a vocabulary name or one specific action. Trade-off: when a case's table has a broad row, a specific request is answered from outside the table |
| 93 | Only the chat fields the server takes | `response_format` off (`NOVA_JSON_MODE=none`); on `INVALID_COMPLETION_FIELDS` the transport drops temperature, then effort, and resends under a new `request_id` |
| 94 | The visit length, revisited | A short visit does not buy efficiency it would otherwise lose, while gathering, communication and tests need the turns: use the turns the plan needs (supports 86) |

## 0.8.0: the evidence review

A physician-level evidence review (kept local) examined the workflow, all 53 playbooks and the note against the contest
requirements, the Korean CPX standards and the clinical evidence.

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 95 | A diagnostic time-out that can still act | The focus call fixed the leading diagnoses after the first history items and nothing heard later could add a question: premature closure by construction. One call (`ReviewPlan`) after the history and review of systems: the 3 leading diagnoses with findings for and against, can't-miss causes not excluded, whether the leader names a cause, up to 3 questions that would most change the ranking (asked at once) and the planned maneuvers ranked, optional ones dropped, up to 2 added. 4 turns are kept for its items |
| 96 | Follow up positive answers | A visit that hears "4 tablets at night" and asks nothing more diagnoses insomnia. Playbook `then` follow-ups (how much, how often, since when, getting worse) are asked right after a positive answer unless the answer already says it; at most 4 per visit |
| 97 | Five explanations, a plan cap of 38 | Explanations count only when spoken: cause, tests, treatment, warning signs, the answer to the worry. Gathering ends at 44; priority-2 items past turn 38 are cut; turns left unused go back, after the time-out, to priority-2 items cut only for turns |
| 98 | The number check reads Korean numerals, line by line | Digits-only comparison rejected a whole correct history ("32" against "thirty-two"). Numbers spoken in words are read as digits, the age and vital signs are added, a difference of two grounded numbers is allowed, and only the failing line is dropped |
| 99 | Neutral prompt examples; the diagnosis names a cause | The assessment prompt's examples had been the sample cases' answers; all are neutral now. The prompt asks whether the diagnosis names a cause and allows one entity; "r/o" and joined diagnoses are stripped. Scores measured on the samples before 0.8.0 overstate private-case accuracy |
| 100 | Plan written per purpose, from linked tests | Playbook tests are `{name, for, why}` and every playbook has a `plan`; P.tests is one line per purpose; follow-up always names warning signs; without the model the note uses the linked tests and the plan |
| 101 | Pelvic exams only when appropriate | Speculum and bimanual exams are `only: sexually_active`: never for a child, for an adolescent only after a "yes", for an adult unless she denied it; model-proposed pelvic exams are checked the same way |
| 102 | Playbook corrections | The audits' errors (a chest-pain "wait 20 minutes" before calling emergency services; an abdominal-pain plan past the cap; outdated statements on male UTI, stool occult blood in acute diarrhea, colorectal screening age) and gaps (high-LR discriminators, red flags at priority 1, safety lines, children's can't-miss causes, Korean epidemiology) were applied in all playbooks |
| 103 | Missing presentations | Against a reconstructed national licensing-exam list, five diagnostic presentations were written: dyslipidemia, oliguria, neck pain, runny or blocked nose, easy bruising. Counselling and visit-type stations need an intervention structure and are not covered |

## 0.8.1: the Doctor model

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 104 | The new Doctor model | The model ID may be omitted, effort is low/medium/high, temperature is ignored, outputs may be long. No model and no temperature on the SDK route; effort stays medium; output limits per call raised about 2.5 times (assessment 16,000), one retry at twice the limit up to 32,000, every call clamped to the case's remaining output budget with room kept for the assessment and the chart |
